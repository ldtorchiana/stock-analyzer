Guide
The 8 Pillars are a quick health scan. Checks and X's don't automatically mean good or bad — it's the story they tell together. Click any pillar for what it means.
Remember: passing pillars ≠ a buy, failing pillars ≠ a sell. The two valuation pillars (5Yr P/E and 5Yr P/FCF) will fail for almost every fast-growing company — that's why the Stock Analyzer exists: to judge whether tomorrow's growth justifies today's price.

The Five Tenets

The principles behind every analysis in this tool.
1We are investors, not speculators
2In the short run the market is a voting machine; long run, a weighing machine
3Every investment is the present value of all future cash flows
4If we don't understand it, we don't invest in it
5A great story can become a bad investment if you pay the wrong price
Educational tool for your own research. Not investment advice.
